# Claims requiring business verification

Do not publish a claim in this list until LYCORE supplies evidence and approves the wording.

## Integrations

- Whether a connection is native, API-based, webhook-based, or uses Zapier, Make, n8n, or custom middleware for each listed platform.
- API access, permissions, rate limits, and supported objects for every field-service, CRM, scheduling, communications, med-spa, and dental platform.
- Official partner, marketplace, certification, or reseller status for any platform.

## Service delivery

- Supported hours, languages, service areas, response targets, live-transfer availability, and escalation rules.
- Specific AI receptionist, SMS, phone, booking, calendar, CRM, and automation capabilities available in a client deployment.
- Healthcare, privacy, security, compliance, or accessibility claims.

## Commercial proof

- Pricing, contract terms, client count, customer names, testimonials, case studies, conversion lifts, revenue results, booking increases, review increases, and rankings.
- Awards, credentials, offices, staff biographies, or client logos.

## Content governance

- Recheck integration details whenever a platform changes its APIs, plans, or policies.
- Use a real author only when the person and credentials have been approved; do not invent expert profiles.
