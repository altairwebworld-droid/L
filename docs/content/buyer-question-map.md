# Buyer question map and first content priorities

The internal question universe contains 168 buyer questions across lead generation, lead response, reputation, CRM automation, websites, integrations, and industry workflows. It is a practical first map for LYCORE's current service scope, not a claim that every question deserves its own page.

## First priorities

1. AI receptionist, missed-call text back, lead follow-up, review automation, CRM versus field-service software, and cold email infrastructure.
2. Pest control, plumbing, roofing, restoration, towing, med spas, dental, and veterinary customer journeys.
3. Verified field-service, CRM, and automation-platform connection methods.

## How to use the map

Cluster questions by the buyer's underlying decision. Publish one thorough guide when it can answer several closely related questions; do not create a near-duplicate page for each wording variation. Before publishing an integration guide, confirm the platform's current connection options and record the evidence in the claims register.

## Priority criteria

Prioritize a topic when it has clear commercial intent, matches a verified LYCORE service, helps a defined industry, can be answered with original practical detail, and does not require an unverified claim. Deprioritize topics that would require fabricated results, partner status, compliance statements, or thin location variants.
