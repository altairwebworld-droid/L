# LYCORE implementation plan

## Existing-page audit

The original site concentrated customer communication, website, and local-search services into the homepage and a broad “What We Build” page. It had no dedicated first-wave service, industry, or integration library. Existing legal, contact, booking, commitment, and company pages are retained.

## Sitemap and content architecture

- Keep: home, what we build, industries, about, contact, booking, FAQ, legal, and commitments.
- Add: eleven service pages, nine industry pages, an integrations hub, and twelve initial integration pages.
- Redirect: legacy towing and bail-bonds industry URLs to the industries hub, as already configured.
- Do not publish case studies, comparisons, or resources until LYCORE has approved facts and useful original material.

## Technical SEO and conversion work

Every new route is included in the shared metadata inventory, static HTML generation, JSON-LD, canonical tags, sitemap, robots rules, internal links, and conversion CTAs. Conversion events are handled by the existing analytics layer. The claims register and search setup runbook document the business confirmations and external steps still required.

## AI-search discoverability

The pages use explicit service, workflow, limitations, control, and integration-relationship language. `robots.txt` allows relevant crawlers and links to `llms.txt`. The site makes no promise of ranking, citation, recommendation, or outcomes.
